About Me

My name is Murad Shah, and I am pursuing dual degrees in Computer Science and Finance. I am deeply passionate about both fields in their entirety, from software engineering and systems design to markets, investment, and financial theory, and most of all the crossover point where the two meet. I am driven by the belief that the future belongs to those who can fluently speak both languages. I also take great pleasure in mentoring my peers and supporting their personal and professional growth. In my free time, I spend a lot of time on sewing and garment-making, a passion I have had since age three, and I swim to keep my mind clear and balanced.
